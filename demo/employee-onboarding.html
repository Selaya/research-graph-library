Employee onboarding — three tracks, one <script> tag

The day an offer is signed, HR, IT and the new hire's manager each start their own track in parallel — three compound containers you can expand to see the steps inside, or collapse to see just the shape of the plan. All three must finish before Day one (an all-join), then a Week 1 check-in closes the loop. Watch what happens when the slow manual steps — a 3-day background check, a 5-day laptop order — get replaced by two integrations: the container's duration chip odometers down and the total-duration bar underneath the graph visibly shrinks.
