The recipe as a DAG — lasagne, sequenced honestly

A recipe card reads top to bottom, but most of it doesn't have to happen that way. Make ragù (a collapsed sub-assembly with a summed duration chip), béchamel, pasta, cheese, and the oven are independent — they only meet at assemble (join:"all"), then bake, then rest. Watch the total-duration bar as the graph goes from one long line, to a parallel DAG, to a jarred-sauce shortcut (g.condense, odometer and all), to assembly itself splitting into its real layers (g.split).
