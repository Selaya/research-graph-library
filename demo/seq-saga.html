Order saga with compensating transactions — one order, two balls: the forward path and its undo

A distributed saga as a sequence diagram: five lifeline columns, one activation per step, every message an edge the run token rides. It charges, reserves, then fails at the shipping step — data.fail reddens that activation and the branch stops dead. The undo cannot continue the same token, so the storyboard adds a fresh root activation (no in-edge) and Mode A seeds a second ball on it, which walks the committed steps back in reverse. Blue is the forward path, amber the compensations.

    What to look for

    • The forward ball dies on book(carrier): a data.fail activation runs its dwell in full, goes red, and fans out to nothing.
    • A second ball appears on saga failed → compensate. It is a new source activation — no in-edge — which is how Mode A seeds a token.
    • Compensations run in reverse order: inventory was reserved last, so it is released first; the charge was taken first, so it is refunded last.
    • Shipping never gets a compensation. You cannot undo a step that never committed.
    • The chips are the pipeline preset reading data.duration; the bar at the bottom of the canvas is the saga's total declared time.
    • Scrub the transport bar backwards: structural steps are snapshotted, so the compensation half folds back up and the diagram is the forward story again.
