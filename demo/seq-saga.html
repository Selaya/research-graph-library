Order saga with compensating transactions — one order, two balls: the forward path and its undo

A distributed saga as a sequence diagram: five lifeline columns, one activation per step, every message an edge the run token rides with its own wire time. It charges, reserves, then fails at the shipping step — data.fail reddens that activation and the branch stops dead, so the arrow leaving it never carries anything. The undo cannot continue a dead token, so the page mints a new one on the orchestrator with run.inject() at the instant the forward ball died, and that second ball walks the committed steps back in reverse. Blue is the forward path, amber the compensations.

    What to look for

    • The forward ball dies on book(carrier): a data.fail activation runs its dwell in full and goes red. The booking failed arrow out of it is drawn, but no token ever crosses it.
    • A second ball appears on saga failed → compensate: run.inject(id) seeds a token there, on the compiled clock, at the moment the run is parked on.
    • Compensations run in reverse order: inventory was reserved last, so it is released first; the charge was taken first, so it is refunded last.
    • Shipping never gets a compensation. You cannot undo a step that never committed.
    • Wires are not all the same length: the carrier call declares edge.data.duration: "150ms" and visibly takes longer to cross than the 6 ms hops inside the cluster.
    • The chips on the boxes are the pipeline preset reading data.duration, the chips on the wires are the edge durations, and the bar at the bottom reports both the declared sum and the critical path.
    • Click any activation to pin its message in the list on the right.
    • Scrub the transport bar backwards: structural steps are snapshotted, so the compensation half folds back up and the diagram is the forward story again.
