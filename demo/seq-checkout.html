Checkout with a 3-D Secure detour — a purchase that has to ask the shopper a question mid-flight

Same solver seam as the login walkthrough: six lifelines, one activation per row, every message an edge the run token rides. This checkout is ordinary until Payment — a third-party service the API calls out to — comes back with a question instead of an answer, so the ball has to fly all the way home to the app before the purchase can continue. Once the shopper clears the challenge, the order completes and the confirmation email fires in the background without the app waiting on it.

    What to look for

    • The ball is one checkout: it dwells on every activation it reaches and always answers back to whoever called it.
    • Every actor is declared container: true, so a lifeline is a dashed, header-only box from the first frame and grows into a column as its activations arrive.
    • Payment's answer to the authorize call isn't a result, it's a question — and that hop is slow in both senses: it skips over Inventory and the API, and the edge declares its own data.duration of 120ms against the 1–3ms calls inside the data centre.
    • The shopper's 0.9s inside the challenge is modeled as its own app activation — the ball just waits there.
    • The order only exists once it lands in Orders DB — that's the moment worth pausing on.
    • The last step fans out two ways at once: the receipt returns to the app while the email fires — nothing waits for the email, and nothing joins the two back up.
