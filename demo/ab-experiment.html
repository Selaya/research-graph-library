A/B experiment lifecycle — hypothesis to ship, with an iteration loop

A hypothesis becomes a design, an implementation, and a three-way fan-out — control, variant A, variant B — each collecting data for two weeks before an all-join rolls them into one analysis. The decision either ships the winner or loops back to redesign, up to three times. Watch the loop's iteration badge tick, and the winning variant tint green while the loser dims.
