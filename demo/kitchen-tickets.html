Restaurant kitchen ticket — fan-out, join, a loop, a failed check

Expo fires one dinner ticket at four stations at once — grill, sauté, fry, garde manger — then plating waits for all four (an all-join) before the pass. The pass is a quality check: usually it sends one plate back to the grill for a quick fix (a bounded retry loop), and once — this run — a steak comes back under and the ticket is declared failed outright (data.fail) instead of looping. Run several tickets: watch the fry station get sped up on its own branch, then step through the plating join one event at a time.
