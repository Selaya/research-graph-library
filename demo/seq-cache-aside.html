Cache-aside: miss, fill, hit — a cold request, a warm one, then a stampede

The same GET /products/42, three times. Cold, the app pays for every hop: gateway to Redis, a miss, Redis to Postgres, and back again to fill the cache. Warm, Redis alone answers. Five minutes later the key expires and two clients race to refill it at once — a cache stampede. The closing beat uses g.condense() to fold request one's miss → fetch → fill chain into a single "read-through" hop.

    What to look for

    • Cold: five hops, ~24ms, most of it one Postgres round trip.
    • Warm: Redis alone answers — three hops, ~2ms.
    • MISS/HIT label what Redis actually decided; the pill on each arrow is the wire call, and the small chip beside it is that wire's own data.duration — 12ms over the internet, 0.2ms inside the VPC.
    • Five minutes on, the key's TTL lapses and two clients miss at once — watch both balls converge on Postgres for the same row.
    • The last beat condenses request one's Redis→Postgres→Redis span into one "read-through cache" hop — same latency, one box. Watch its duration chip and the total-duration bar at the bottom (sum and critical path, from the pipeline preset).
