    What to look for

    • Two balls leave the gateway at once. “call search · 2s timer” fans out to the RPC and to its own timeout — that is what a client-side timeout really is, a race, so both activations share one row.
    • The red activation retries itself. data.fail carries the reason and the onFail arc out of it carries the budget, so the lookup runs its full 2 s dwell four times — one fail event per attempt, and only the last one terminal.
    • The big arc on the right is the retry edge — loop:true, onFail:true, maxIterations 3, backoff 200 → 400 → 800 ms. Attempt 2 re-flies it; attempts 3 and 4 tick in place, and the iter n/3 badge on the arc is the count.
    • The duration chips are the point. The pipeline preset prints the declared duration on every activation — two 2s timeouts against the probe's 38ms — and on every message, from a 1ms call inside the datacentre to the 40ms hop out to the phone.
    • The blank row before “tap Search again” is a gap, and the message that crosses it declares data.duration: "30s" — thirty seconds of wire time, drawn as a long crossing over empty lifeline.
    • The Search lifeline goes red and then clears. The actor container carries statusAgg: "latest", so the column follows the most recent outcome of its activations instead of keeping the first failure for the rest of the run.
    • Click any activation — g.on('nodeclick') selects its line in the message list.
    • Scrub the transport bar backwards — structural steps are snapshotted, so the diagram folds back up and the breaker re-opens.
