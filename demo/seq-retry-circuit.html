    What to look for

    • Two balls leave the gateway at once. “call search · 2s timer” fans out to the RPC and to its own timeout — that is what a client-side timeout really is, a race, so both activations share one row.
    • The red activation is terminal. data.fail runs the full 2 s dwell and then stops the branch: no finish event, no fan-out, no loop.
    • The big arc on the right is the retry edge — loop:true, maxIterations 3, backoff 200 → 400 → 800 ms. Attempt 2 re-flies it; attempts 3 and 4 tick in place on “call search”, and the iter n/3 badge on the arc is the visible count.
    • The duration chips are the point. The pipeline preset prints the declared duration on every activation — two 2s timeouts against the probe's 38ms.
    • The blank row before “tap Search again” is a gap: thirty seconds of nothing, drawn as empty lifeline.
    • The Search lifeline stays red even after the probe succeeds: a container inherits the earliest failure among its descendants, so the column carries the timeout for the rest of the run.
    • Scrub the transport bar backwards — structural steps are snapshotted, so the diagram folds back up and the breaker re-opens.
