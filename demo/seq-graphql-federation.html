GraphQL federation — parallel subgraphs, and the N+1 a DataLoader batches away

One query — {me{cart{items{product{name reviews{rating}}}}}} — through a federated gateway and three subgraphs. It fans out to Users and Products at once, joins both with join:"all", then asks Reviews once per product: three round trips. The last beat merges those five activations into one g.condense()d reviews(products:[1,2,3]) and replays the leg.

    What to look for

    • One row carries two activations: the fan-out to Users and Products really is simultaneous, so two balls cross at once.
    • join:"all" on “merge entities” makes the gateway wait for both before it moves on.
    • Beat 2 zigzags: gateway → Reviews → gateway, three times. That shape is the N+1.
    • Each arrow carries its own wire time (edge.data.duration): the 1ms in-cluster calls flick across, the 9ms cross-region fetches to Reviews visibly take their time.
    • The condense set includes the two gateway waits — without them a path would leave the set and re-enter, which condense() rejects as non-convex.
    • After the merge, the chip odometer rolls 117ms → 45ms and the total-duration bar drops.
