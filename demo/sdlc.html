SDLC release pipeline — from manual to automated — one <script> tag, one storyboard

A mobile/TV release, end to end: plan → develop → build & sign → QA → release, as five compound phases across three device types (iOS, Android, Smart TV). The story assembles the pipeline, runs one release the way it works today — a code-review retry loop ticking in place, a signing fan-out, a regression fan-out at three different speeds into an all-join, a deploy fan-out into GA — then lands automation on three of those areas and re-runs it. Watch the duration chips odometer (11h → 45s, 7d → 12m, 7h → 4m) and the total-duration bar under the graph shrink. Play, pause and scrub with the transport bar, or jump straight to a chapter below.
