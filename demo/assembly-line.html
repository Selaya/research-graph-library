The assembly line — sub-assemblies, a QC fail, and a rework loop

Three sub-assemblies — chassis, drivetrain, electronics — build as collapsed containers with a summed duration chip, converge on an join:"all" final assembly, and hit QC inspection. QC is declared as a retry: data.fail = { reason, recover: true }, with the dashed rework arc marked onFail: true, maxIterations: 2. Every failed inspection rides that arc back to final assembly; the attempt that spends the last rework passes, and the line moves on to packaging and shipping.
